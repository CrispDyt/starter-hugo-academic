I am a PhD candidate at the **Translational Health Research Institute (THRI)** at **Western Sydney University**. I completed my undergraduate studies at **Beihang University (BUAA)**, earning a bachelor's degree in **engineering** and **economics**. I then obtained my master's degree in **Mathematics** from the **University of Macau (UM)**, and my PhD is partially funded by the **Western Sydney Primary Health Network**.

My research focuses on **dynamical systems**, in particular the **information loss** that arises when nonlinear dynamical systems are only partially observed. Using tools from **measure-theoretic optimal transport**, I have found answers to a number of interesting questions in this setting, such as quantifying the information loss of partially observed systems, selecting the optimal observability, and resolving the information loss caused by long-term operator rollouts through **recurrence graph measures**. On the application side, I am interested in **stochastic optimal control** problems in **system dynamics modelling**. I am currently developing **measure-theoretic test-time recursive self-improvement** models, which I hope to apply to predicting **temporally sparse patient trajectories**.
